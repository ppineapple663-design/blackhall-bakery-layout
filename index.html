Unit 4, 11-15 Blackhall Street, Paisley PA1 1TD

Persian bakery: proposed layout

A production bakery for sangak, barbari, taftoon and lavash, trading by delivery and collection only. Customers and riders come in through the shared yard to a small pick-up counter just inside the entrance. There's a minimal display and no eating on site.

      Notes

      • Drawn from the site plan (October 2022) and six site photographs taken on 10 November 2026. The unit's shape and entrance position follow the site plan. Dimensions and the cast-iron column positions are approximate and need confirming by a measured survey.
      • The only entrance is on the east side, off the shared yard, so customers, riders and flour deliveries all use it. Deliveries are timed outside collection hours.
      • No second exit is shown. Building control will confirm whether one is needed, based on travel distance to the entrance.
      • Fit-out finishes: hygienic white wall cladding, a resin floor and a suspended ceiling (left off the model so the inside is visible). The sangak oven, deck oven and tanoor sit under one extraction canopy with a gas interlock.
      • Staff WC is reached through a lobby, with no door opening directly into a food room. Subject to building warrant, planning (use class) and environmental health registration with Renfrewshire Council.
