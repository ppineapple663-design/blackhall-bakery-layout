Unit 4, 11-15 Blackhall Street, Paisley PA1 1TD

Persian bakery: proposed layout

A production bakery for sangak, barbari, taftoon and lavash, trading by delivery and collection only. There is a small pick-up counter with a minimal display, and no eating on site.

      Notes

      • Drawn from six site photographs on 10 November 2026. Dimensions, the cast-iron column positions and the door positions are approximate, and need confirming by a measured survey.
      • Fit-out finishes shown: hygienic white wall cladding, a resin floor and a suspended ceiling (left off the model so the inside is visible).
      • The sangak oven, deck oven and tanoor sit under one extraction canopy, with a gas interlock, ducted through the roof.
      • The rear door is proposed for flour deliveries and as a fire exit, subject to a building warrant. Staff WC is reached through a lobby, with no door opening directly into a food room.
      • Subject to building warrant, planning (use class) and environmental health registration with Renfrewshire Council.
